Ironhold

Walled forge-town · ~10,000 souls · Southern Aelorian Territories · Member city, Aelorian union

Three concentric ringed wards around a central gold-domed civic core. Eight districts, two gates. Rocky foothills, four days south of Aeloria. Celestial Plateau visible on southern horizon on clear days.

Governed locally by the Forge Council — 9 seats, 7 guild masters + 2 civic. Represented on the Aelorian Council by dwarven envoy Vorik Stonestrong (resident in Aeloria for 6 years, has not visited Ironhold in that time).

Patron god: Korvain. Every home has a hearth-shrine. Every foundry has a full one.

Northern Gate
Session 6 arrival
Smaller, plainer, older than the Southern Gate. Faces the Aeloria road. Handles nearly all daily traffic — Aelorian merchants, refugees, and recently, the Aurum Dominion diplomatic party. Manned round the clock by the Ironhold Watch's professional guards.
DM: Party arrives here at dawn on Day 12 (25 Frostfall). Brenna parts ways here to reach the merchants and explain what happened to the caravan. Sera peels off toward her sister. The Watch gate captain is polite but curious about the wagon-loss story.
Southern Gate
ceremonial · Celestium
The grand trade gate. Twin bastion towers, portcullis, ceremonial approach. Faces the road up to the Celestial Plateau. Celestium Clan dwarven caravans, southern mineral shipments, and ceremonial plateau delegations all enter here — the grandeur is a nod to the ancient dwarven connection.
DM: The party won't likely use this gate unless they travel south. If Halvor tries to flee Ironhold, this is one route.

Anvilhome Ward
District 2
Smaller outer gold-domed building — the Anvilhome, temple to Korvain. Priests' quarters, civic archives, and the ceremonial forge where new Council members are inducted. Quieter than the Council Ward. Older.
The Anvilhome
Priestess Elisara · High Priestess of Korvain
Temple to Korvain. Ceremonial forge for Council inductions. Elisara is guardian of the Heart of Ironhold, held in a reliquary chamber under the dome. She wears the iron key to the chamber on a leather thong at all times.

Foundry Row
District 3
The heart of Ironhold's industry. Rows of foundries, master smithies, finishing shops. Heaviest smoke plumes in the town. Streets slick with soot, quiet at midnight, roaring by dawn.
The Grand Foundry
Guildmaster Bryndis Ashveil · Blades guild
Bryndis's operation. The largest and most respected foundry in the row. Senior Council member, unofficial leader of the reformist faction. Refuses the Aurum contract. Losing the political battle to Drake.
reformistBlades guild
Drake's Furnace
Corvan Drake · Ingots guild
Drake's operation. Younger, ambitious. Wants the Aurum contract — sees Ironhold's future in it. More energy and more money to spread than Bryndis. Framed Kellan for cult activity to remove a competitor's fixer.
Drake factionframed Kellan
DM: Drake does not know Kellan is Guilded Veil. He thinks he framed an ambitious rival smith's fixer. He does not perceive the wider political consequences of the Aurum deal.
Merchants' Quarter
District 4
Main markets, mercantile inns, guild halls, moneylenders' offices. Trade contracts signed here; most non-forge business happens here. Stays open late.
The Grey Kettle
Mother Hessa · 28 years running
Ironhold's premier merchant inn. Neutral ground. Clean rooms. Proper wine. Where contracts get signed and where the party would most naturally set up if they want to be seen.

The Stonecut Ward
District 5
Ironhold's dwarven quarter — ~800 dwarves and half-dwarves, plus families. Cliff-face workshops carved into the western rocks. Runs to jewellery, mail, precision work, and stone.
The Ashfall
Grust · 91 years running
The Stonecut Ward's inn. Dwarven-favoured. Quiet. Deep cellar. Excellent ale. Outsiders welcome by introduction only. Dwarven-bread yeast, malt, hearth-stone — you can hear yourself think.

Rustpocket
District 6
Residential for foundry workers, transient labourers, and everyone Ironhold needs but doesn't want to see. Timber tenements, narrow streets, cheap beer. Guilded Veil cell operates here (from a safehouse, currently sheltering Kellan).
The Broken Anvil
Riven · 11 years running
Cheapest bed in town. Loudest crowd. Rough house rules applied evenly. If the party wants information nobody else will share, this is the place — but they'll be watched from the door onward.

The Slag
District 7
The worst area of Ironhold. Fringe by the outer wall where the acid runoff drains, where the poorest live, where the town's problems are quietly dumped. The Watch doesn't patrol here after dark unless they must.
Warehouse #14
Operated (secretly) by Wesley Halloran · Coin Cipher way-station
Officially a mid-tier merchant's bulk-goods storage. Actually a Coin Cipher way-station. The chest was supposed to arrive here. Wesley is expecting delivery.

Outside the walls agricultural
Farmhold Reach
District 8
Patchwork farmland northeast of the town. Small tenant farms, animal pens, cottage smallholds. Feeds Ironhold. Not walled, not guarded — the Watch runs occasional patrols but the reach is effectively open. When Ironhold is anxious, farmhold families move inside the walls; the walls have not needed to close in living memory.
